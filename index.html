02 / OUR APPROACH

Understand the language before building the solution.

Real-world language is messy. People switch between languages, use regional expressions, write words phonetically, and vary their spelling from one message to the next.

We are interested in understanding these variations through language data, model evaluation, and experimentation with multilingual AI techniques.

A

Data and representation

Explore how multilingual and code-mixed text can be represented and evaluated.

B

Models and evaluation

Investigate model behavior across language combinations, scripts, and spelling variants.

C

Practical applications

Work toward useful AI applications that reflect how multilingual communities communicate.

03 / ABOUT BHASHA LABS

A long-term interest in language-aware AI.

Bhasha Labs is an independent, early-stage technology initiative exploring practical AI for India's multilingual environment.

We are beginning with code-mixed language understanding and Romanized Indic text, with a broader interest in making AI more useful across languages and communication styles.

Our current work is exploratory. We aim to develop this direction through research, experimentation, and iterative prototyping.
