Starting with code-mixed language.

Real conversations don't always stay inside one language.

A message can move between English, Hindi, Gujarati, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, or another language within the same sentence — and the same Indic word can be written in many different ways.

We are exploring models and datasets that can better understand this kind of natural multilingual communication.
